Triangle Research

What if your organisation was built the way the Trinity is? Three distinct voices. Unified in one. It is the oldest structure there is, and nobody has ever run a company on it.

Three decide. The dissent is kept.

Three people decide, not nine. Each says where they stand, in their own words, and it is written down. The framework is free and you can start on Monday.

The problem

Nine people go into a room. Every one of them has a view. Ninety minutes later there is one view — and it is not the best one in the room, it is the one everybody could live with.

Nobody can tell you afterwards who believed what.

Whoever speaks first sets the range everyone else argues inside. The thing that only one person knows never gets said, because saying it means interrupting. And by minute twenty everybody is arguing about the same narrow question, which was never the important one.

So when it fails, eighteen months later, you go back to find out why — and there is a deck, an outcome, and a date. That is everything.

Why three

Rulers decide fast and crush dissent. Committees hear everyone and move nothing. You have sat in both.

Two people deadlock. Four people deadlock. Three cannot.

Three is the smallest group that can hold real disagreement and is structurally incapable of failing to resolve it. Three positions, two options, no possible tie — which is also why there is no neutral, no abstention and no coming back to you on that. A neutral is the one thing that could make three people fail to reach an answer.

How it works

  1. The proposition goes out before anyone meets. One sentence somebody can agree or disagree with. Writing it properly is the hardest part, and it is the part most companies have never done.
  2. Three people are named for it. Not the org chart. Whoever actually knows, and whoever has to live with it. The reason each person is there is written on the page. Everyone else carries on working.
  3. They argue. Exactly the way they always have. This part is not touched. It is the part that works.
  4. Each states a position out loud. Agree, or disagree. Nothing else. In their own words, recorded as spoken — not summarised, not minuted. And you do not have to know why to say no.
  5. Two of three carries it. The one who disagreed stays in the record, named, in their own words, next to the decision it lost to.
The claim

The majority decides. The minority survives. No other way of deciding anything can say both of those.

What it costs your meeting

Nothing before it. About three minutes inside it. A record after it.

There is no software to install, no licence, no procurement and nothing to roll out. There is a habit to maintain, and habits are the expensive part — which is the honest version of that sentence.

What comes out

  • One page per decision: the proposition, the three, what each of them said, and who carried it
  • The dissent preserved by name, in the words it was spoken in
  • A record of who was named on what, and why
  • Across a quarter: how often anybody in your company disagrees out loud — a question no executive has ever been able to answer

Corrections are appended, never overwritten. A record that can be quietly edited is not a record.

The room is closed

The record always exists. The distribution list varies, and the list itself is part of the record. Some decisions cannot go to everybody — a restriction that is written down stays rare, and one that is silent becomes a habit.

What Triangle does not do

  • It does not remove anyone's authority. Whoever could overrule still can — and the overrule is recorded next to what was known at the time
  • It does not work with two people. If there is genuinely no third, it is one person deciding with an adviser. Say so rather than dress it up
  • It does not measure anybody. Nothing here belongs in a performance review, and putting it there destroys the record inside a quarter
  • It does not promise better outcomes. Nobody in this category can prove that, including the forty-year-old clinical version of the same structure
  • It does not stop three people being confidently wrong together

Who it is for

Companies at the point where decisions have outgrown one person. The founder or chief executive can no longer be in everything, and knows it.

Under Triangle their job changes shape: they sit on the decisions where they carry the consequence, they read every record, they choose who carries what, and they overrule visibly when they must. That is a bigger job than attending, and a less comfortable one. Some find it a relief.
