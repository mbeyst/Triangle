## Phase 2: Comment Strategy (Weeks 2-4)

**Goal:** Become a recognizable, valuable voice before pitching.

### The Approach

**DON'T:**
- "Check out my framework!"
- "This is like Triangle, which I invented..."
- Link-dropping in first comment

**DO:**
- Add a specific insight from Triangle principles
- Reference the concept without naming it
- Build recognition over 3-5 interactions

### Comment Templates

**Template 1: Add a Pattern**
> "This reminds me of a pattern I've seen: when [X] is the decision, 2/3 agreement moves faster than consensus, but you need the dissent recorded. Otherwise the veto person blocks execution later."

**Template 2: Ask a Question**
> "Curious: how did you handle the person who disagreed? In triads we use '2/3 moves, 1/3 records' — dissent is documented but doesn't block. Did you try something similar?"

**Template 3: Share a Result**
> "We tested this with a tech stack decision. 45 minutes, 3 people, one dissenter on record. Ship date hit because the dissenter's concern was tracked, not debated to death. Interesting dynamic."

**Template 4: Reference Without Pitching**
> "The dyad/triad distinction matters here. This feels like a triad decision (affects others, resource commitment) but you're running it as a dyad. That's why it's stuck."

### Sequencing

| Interaction | Action | Goal |
|-------------|--------|------|
| 1 | Thoughtful comment on their post | Get on their radar |
| 2 | Reply to their reply (if they engage) | Build rapport |
| 3 | Comment on another post (different topic) | Show consistency |
| 4 | Share their content with your take | Reciprocity |
| 5 | DM or pitch | Now you're a known voice |

**Timeline:** 3-4 weeks of commenting before any pitch

## Phase 3: Podcast Pitch Strategy (Weeks 5-8)

### The Angle

**DON'T pitch:**
- "I have a decision framework called Triangle"
- "Let me explain the theology behind it"
- "Here's why it's better than other frameworks"

**DO pitch:**
- "Humans decide, AI facilitates — we built a tool that does this"
- "3-person triads with 2/3 agreement move faster than consensus, preserve integrity better than autocracy"
- "We've seen dissent recorded (not blocked) actually improve execution because objections are tracked"

### Pitch Templates

**Template 1: Cold Email/DM**

```
Subject: Triangle — decision framework with AI facilitation

Hi [Name],

I've been following your work on [specific topic they cover]. Your episode on [specific episode] shaped how we think about [specific insight].

I'm reaching out because we've built something that fits your audience:

Triangle is a decision architecture where:
- 3-person triads make significant decisions (not individuals, not whole teams)
- 2/3 agreement moves the decision forward (no veto power)
- 1/3 dissent is recorded and published (not suppressed)
- AI facilitates (tracks equity, summarizes, logs decisions) — but doesn't decide

We've seen this cut decision time from weeks to <60 minutes while preserving dissent (which actually improves execution).

I'd love to come on [Podcast Name] and talk about:
- Why triads beat both autocracy and consensus
- How AI facilitation works without making the decision
- What we learned from [X] triads using this in the wild

No pitch for the tool — just the pattern and what it reveals about group decision-making.

Are you open to guests on this topic?

Best,
Martin
[LinkedIn profile]
[triangle.systems — once deployed]
```

## Phase 4: Content Seeding (Ongoing)

### LinkedIn Posts (Your Platform)

**Post Type 1: Pattern Observation**
```
Noticed something in triad decisions:

When dissent is recorded (not debated to death), the dissenter executes better.

Why? Their objection is on the record. If they're right, they're vindicated later. If they're wrong, the record shows why.

Both outcomes improve execution.

2/3 moves. 1/3 records.
```

**Post Type 2: Case Study**
```
Tech stack decision. 3 people. 45 minutes.

CTO: "Svelte is faster."
Lead Dev: "React means we can hire."
Product: "Just ship in Q3."

Decision: React + 2-week Svelte prototype.
Dissent recorded: CTO's objection on technical debt.

Outcome: Prototype showed 35% reduction. Hiring concern validated. Decision stood.

Dissent wasn't suppressed. It was tracked. And it led to a review date.

This is how triads work.
```

**Post Type 3: Theological Honesty**
```
Triangle draws on Social Trinitarianism.

Three distinct persons, yet inseparable in essence.
Each voice matters, but the group decides together.
No hierarchy of essence.

I'm stating the source openly. This isn't faith branding — it's intellectual honesty.

The framework works for believers and non-believers alike. But I won't pretend the inspiration came from nowhere.
```
