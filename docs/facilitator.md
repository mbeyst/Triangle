# Facilitator Guide

**Frame it. Vote. Record. Move.**

## Human Facilitator Checklist

If you're facilitating a triad session manually:

### Before the Session

- [ ] Confirm the decision to be made (write it in one sentence)
- [ ] Invite three participants (diverse perspectives, relevant competence)
- [ ] Set timebox (30-60 minutes)
- [ ] Prepare decision log template

### During the Session

- [ ] Open with the decision statement (read it aloud)
- [ ] Ensure each person speaks first without interruption (2-3 minutes each)
- [ ] Track speaking time (alert if one person exceeds 50% of airtime)
- [ ] Surface areas of agreement
- [ ] Surface areas of disagreement
- [ ] Call the decision when 2/3 agreement is clear

### After the Session

- [ ] Publish decision summary within 24 hours
- [ ] Include dissenting views (verbatim if possible)
- [ ] Note what would change the dissenter's mind
- [ ] Schedule review date if decision is reversible

---

## AI Facilitator

The AI facilitator automates the above. It:

1. **Records inputs** — each voice captured separately
2. **Tracks equity** — alerts if speaking time unbalanced
3. **Aggregates** — surfaces patterns across inputs
4. **Publishes** — generates decision summary
5. **Flags dissent** — makes disagreement visible, not hidden

### Sample AI Prompts

**Opening:**
> "Welcome to this Triangle triad session. The decision we're facilitating is: [DECISION]. Each person will have 2 minutes to share their initial thoughts without interruption. I'll track time and ensure equity. Let's begin with [NAME]."

**Mid-session:**
> "I'm noticing we've spent 15 minutes on this topic. [NAME], you've spoken 3 times; [NAME], you've spoken once. Before we move forward, I'd like to hear from [QUIET PERSON]."

**Closing:**
> "Here's what I'm hearing: [SUMMARY OF AGREEMENT]. [NAME] and [NAME] are aligned on [X]. [NAME] has concerns about [Y]. By the Triangle rule, 2/3 agreement moves the decision forward. The dissent is recorded as: [VERBATIM OBJECTION]. Shall I publish this summary?"

## Decision Log Template

```markdown
## Decision: [One sentence]

**Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
**Triad:** [Name 1], [Name 2], [Name 3]
**Facilitator:** [Human/AI name]

### Consensus
[What 2/3 agreed on]

### Dissent
[Name]'s objection:
> [Verbatim or near-verbatim]

### Conditions that would change this decision
[If reversible, what would trigger review?]

### Review date
[YYYY-MM-DD or "N/A - irreversible"]
```
