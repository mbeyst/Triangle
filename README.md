# Triangle

**Three people. One decision. Every voice heard.**

Triangle is a decision architecture for significant choices. It uses triads (three-person groups) with AI facilitation to reach decisions that preserve both speed and integrity.

---

## The Problem

Most organisations oscillate between two dysfunctional modes:

1. **Speed over inclusion** — one person decides, others resist during execution
2. **Inclusion over speed** — everyone must agree, nothing moves

Triangle offers a third way: structured triadic decision-making that moves on 2/3 agreement while recording (not blocking) dissent.

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Triads for significant decisions only

Not every choice needs three people. Use:

- **Dyads (2 people)** — collaboration, execution, low-stakes choices
- **Triads (3 people)** — strategic decisions, resource allocation, hiring, direction

**Threshold:** If the decision affects more than the decider's own work, it's triad territory.

### 2. 2/3 moves, 1/3 records

- **2 agree** — decision moves forward
- **1 dissents** — objection is recorded, published, not blocked

This prevents both tyranny of the majority and tyranny of the veto.

### 3. AI facilitates, humans decide

The AI facilitator:
- Records each voice separately
- Tracks speaking time (alerts if one dominates)
- Aggregates inputs
- Publishes consensus
- Flags where disagreement remains

The AI does **not** make the decision. Authority morphs to competence, not algorithm.

## Theological Grounding

Triangle draws on **Social Trinitarianism** — the doctrine that God is three distinct persons, yet inseparable in essence.

**Key parallels:**

- **Distinct, yet inseparable** — each voice matters, but the group decides together
- **Perichoresis (mutual indwelling)** — decisions emerge from relationship, not aggregation
- **No hierarchy of essence** — each member has equal weight

This is not faith branding. It's intellectual honesty about the source.

## When to Use Triangle

| Situation | Use Triad? | Why |
|-----------|------------|-----|
| Hiring a team member | ✓ | Affects team dynamics |
| Choosing a tech stack | ✓ | Long-term architectural impact |
| Daily standup decisions | ✗ | Dyad or individual suffices |
| Budget allocation >€10k | ✓ | Resource commitment |
| Content approval | ✗ | Dyad (creator + reviewer) |
| Strategic pivot | ✓ | Directional, affects all |

## Quick Start

1. **Identify the decision** — is it significant enough for a triad?
2. **Gather three people** — diverse perspectives, relevant competence
3. **Set a timebox** — 30-60 minutes max for most decisions
4. **AI facilitator joins** — records, tracks, summarizes
5. **Decision emerges** — 2/3 agreement moves it forward
6. **Dissent published** — objections documented, not suppressed
